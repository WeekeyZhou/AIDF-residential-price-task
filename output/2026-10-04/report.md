### 3-Month Compounded SORA

**Why it is included.** Three-month compounded SORA is the reference rate for most floating-rate Singapore home loans, so it moves monthly instalments and the amount buyers can borrow under the Total Debt Servicing Ratio. Falling SORA raises purchasing power and supports sale prices with a lag of a few quarters. The effect on rents is ambiguous (cheaper mortgages pull tenants into buying but also lower landlords' costs), so it is not used as a rental signal.

**Definition.** Singapore Overnight Rate Average compounded over the preceding three months, end of month, published by MAS. SORA is the volume-weighted average rate of unsecured overnight interbank SGD transactions.

**Source.** MONETARY AUTHORITY OF SINGAPORE; table M700071, series 23 "Compounded Singapore Overnight Rate Average (SORA) - 3 Month". Unit: Per Cent Per Annum. Observed: monthly. Source last updated: 14/09/2026.

### Consumer Price Index, All Items (2024 = 100)

**Why it is included.** General inflation anchors nominal rents and construction and replacement costs, and it is the input MAS reacts to when setting policy, which feeds back into interest rates. **Definition.** Price index of a fixed basket of goods and services bought by resident households, not seasonally adjusted, published by DOS.

### Private Residential Vacancy Rate

**Why it is included.** Vacancy measures slack in the existing stock. Landlords cut asking rents when units sit empty, so a rising vacancy rate is the most direct leading signal for rents, and with a longer lag for sale prices.

**Definition.** Vacant completed private homes divided by all completed private homes, times 100. Computed by this workflow from the two URA series.
